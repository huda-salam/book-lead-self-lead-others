# Chapter 25 QA — Dari Keputusan ke Eksekusi

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Execution is treated as leadership work connecting decisions to observable outcomes.

2. **Boundary with Chapter 24 — PASS**  
   Choice and trade-offs remain Chapter 24; Chapter 25 begins after a direction has been selected.

3. **Boundary with Chapter 26 — PASS**  
   Chapter 26 owns outcome review; Chapter 25 includes only operational checkpoints and adjustment triggers.

4. **Mechanism — PASS**  
   The chapter separates decision, owner, outcome, plan, dependencies, execution, monitoring, adjustment, and escalation.

5. **Ownership — PASS**  
   A clear accountable owner is required without implying that the owner must perform every task.

6. **Activity versus outcome — PASS**  
   The chapter explicitly warns against treating meetings, completed tasks, or busyness as proof of outcome progress.

7. **Dependency/coordination — PASS**  
   Handoffs, approvals, data, capacity, and external dependencies are included as execution variables.

8. **Adaptive execution — PASS**  
   Method changes are allowed when evidence changes, while silent abandonment of commitments is distinguished from legitimate adaptation.

9. **Monitoring — PASS**  
   Leading indicators are introduced as early signals, without claiming they universally predict outcomes.

10. **Escalation — PASS**  
    Escalation is framed as part of execution design rather than an automatic failure.

11. **People/system diagnosis — PASS**  
    When execution stalls, the chapter checks people, process, priority, dependency, decision, resource, and assumptions before assigning blame.

12. **Practical framework — PASS**  
    `Decision → Owner → Outcome → Plan → Dependencies → Execute → Monitor → Adjust → Escalate/Continue` is explicitly labeled as a book synthesis, not a validated taxonomy.

13. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Implementation-intention evidence is used for concrete enactment, not as proof that a planning card guarantees organizational execution.

14. **Red-team — PASS WITH MINOR PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for implementation, monitoring, coordination, and adaptive execution claims.

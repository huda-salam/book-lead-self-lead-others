# Chapter 26 QA — Ketika Keputusan Tidak Menghasilkan Outcome yang Diinginkan

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Review is framed as a leadership capability: learning from consequences without collapsing into blame or self-defense.

2. **Boundary with Chapter 24 — PASS**  
   Chapter 24 owns choice and trade-offs. Chapter 26 reviews the decision after outcome evidence exists.

3. **Boundary with Chapter 25 — PASS**  
   Chapter 25 owns operational execution. Chapter 26 distinguishes execution problems from decision and context problems.

4. **Boundary with Chapter 23 — PASS**  
   Chapter 23 owns causal reasoning. Chapter 26 uses causal distinctions only as a review aid and does not claim a validated attribution taxonomy.

5. **Hindsight discipline — PASS**  
   The chapter explicitly reconstructs what was knowable at decision time and separates later information from prior knowledge.

6. **Decision versus outcome — PASS**  
   Poor outcome is not treated as automatic proof of poor decision; good outcome is not treated as automatic proof of sound process.

7. **Reflection versus rumination — PASS**  
   Review is structured around observable gaps and updates rather than repetitive self-criticism.

8. **Accountability — PASS**  
   Learning is not used to erase responsibility for negligence, rule violations, or misconduct.

9. **Persistence/disengagement — PASS**  
   Continue/change/stop is tied to goal validity, evidence, assumptions, constraints, and future value rather than sunk cost.

10. **Practical framework — PASS**  
    The review sequence is explicitly labeled as a book synthesis, not a validated taxonomy.

11. **Anti-analysis-paralysis — PASS**  
    Review ends in an update or next decision rather than indefinite retrospective analysis.

12. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for hindsight/decision quality distinctions, self-regulation/feedback, reflection versus rumination, and persistence/disengagement.

## Editorial verdict

**PASS. Part V complete. Proceed to Part VI, Chapter 27.**

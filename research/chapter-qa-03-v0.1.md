# Chapter 3 QA — v0.1

## QA result

**Overall: PASS WITH REVISIONS**

Chapter 3 establishes `SELF.HUMILITY` as a capability rather than an identity and correctly distinguishes humility from low self-esteem, passivity, agreement, and lack of accountability.

## Strong points

- Clear canonical ownership of humility.
- Strong connection to correction, evidence, and updating beliefs.
- Avoids “humility = always yield”.
- Includes both subordinate and leader perspectives.
- The distinction between source/status and content validity is useful.
- The practice is behavior-linked rather than presented as a validated intervention.

## Evidence

The evidence map supports cautious claims about humble leadership and follower/relational outcomes, while explicitly warning about construct overlap and causal limitations.

The chapter correctly avoids:
- “humility guarantees leadership success”;
- “humility causes performance” as a universal claim;
- treating humility as a single settled construct.

## Required revisions

### 1. Broaden the opening evidence claim

The current sentence about meta-analysis findings is accurate in direction but should explicitly identify that the evidence concerns **humble leadership**, not humility in every context.

Recommended wording:

> “Penelitian tentang humble leadership menemukan hubungan dengan sejumlah outcome relasional dan perilaku…”

This is already close; retain this scope consistently.

### 2. “Status membuat humility lebih sulit”

Useful as a practical hypothesis, but currently reads somewhat like a universal organizational law.

Revise toward:

> “Posisi dengan otoritas dapat membuat orang lain lebih enggan menyampaikan koreksi secara langsung.”

Then frame the consequence as a practical risk, not a universal effect.

### 3. “Pisahkan sumber dari isi”

This is a strong heuristic, but should not imply source characteristics are irrelevant.

Expertise, access to information, and track record can be relevant evidence. The intended rule is:

> status alone should not substitute for examining the claim.

### 4. Add an explicit boundary on updating

The chapter should state:

> “Bersedia berubah bukan berarti harus berubah setiap kali ada pendapat baru.”

This protects humility from becoming suggestibility.

### 5. Keep “wibawa” formulation as synthesis

The claim that admitting mistakes can contribute to credibility is plausible and useful, but should remain a book inference/practice principle unless a specific evidence record is added.

## Canonical ownership

Chapter 3 owns:
- humility as capability;
- accurate self-view;
- openness to correction;
- contribution recognition;
- willingness to update.

It must not absorb:
- detailed observation/interpretation framework → Ch7;
- detailed perspective-taking → Ch8;
- respect as a full interpersonal construct → Ch11;
- listening mechanics → Ch10;
- discipline of evidence → LITERACY.DISCIPLINE / Ch7.

## Practice QA

“Receive Correction” is well aligned with the mechanism:

**listen → restate → ask → separate → update**

Important boundary:

> The practice trains openness to correction; it does not prescribe accepting every criticism.

“Find the Contribution” should remain explicitly a pedagogical drill, not an empirically validated intervention.

## Red-team

### Does humility become self-belittling?
No.

### Does humility become passivity?
No.

### Does humility require agreement?
No.

### Does humility mean believing every critic?
No.

### Does humility mean changing one's mind constantly?
Not currently explicit enough. Add the boundary.

### Does humility become moral superiority?
The chapter explicitly warns against this.

### Does status become irrelevant?
Potential risk. Add the distinction that status/expertise can be relevant evidence, but status alone is not proof.

## Retrieval QA

Useful entry points:

### Questions
- “Bagaimana menerima koreksi tanpa kehilangan wibawa?”
- “Apakah rendah hati berarti selalu mengalah?”
- “Bagaimana tahu bahwa saya memang tidak tahu?”
- “Kapan saya harus mengubah pendapat?”
- “Bagaimana membedakan humility dengan rendah diri?”

### Cases
- bawahan mengoreksi atasan;
- junior menemukan kesalahan senior;
- keputusan lama berhasil berkali-kali tetapi kondisi berubah;
- kritik benar sebagian dan salah sebagian.

### Red flags
- “Saya sudah tahu.”
- “Dia masih junior.”
- “Saya harus membuktikan saya benar.”
- “Kalau saya mengaku salah, wibawa saya hilang.”

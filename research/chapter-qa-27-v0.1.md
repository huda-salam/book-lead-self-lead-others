# Chapter 27 QA — Organisasi Bukan Hanya Struktur Resmi

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Chapter trains system reading rather than generic organizational theory.

2. **Formal/informal distinction — PASS**  
   Org chart is treated as a useful but incomplete representation of organizational reality.

3. **Mechanism — PASS**  
   Structure, roles, workflows, information, incentives, norms, networks, decisions, and feedback are connected as a practical diagnostic map.

4. **Informal network boundary — PASS**  
   Informal influence is neither romanticized nor automatically treated as political manipulation.

5. **System versus individual accountability — PASS**  
   System explanations do not erase individual responsibility.

6. **Local optimization — PASS**  
   Cross-unit incentives and handoffs are used to explain system-level side effects without claiming that every conflict is structural.

7. **Decision rights — PASS**  
   Authority, escalation, and bottlenecks are included without duplicating Chapter 28's focus on power and influence.

8. **Culture/norm caution — PASS**  
   The chapter avoids treating “culture” as an all-purpose explanation and asks what structural or incentive mechanisms may sustain observed behavior.

9. **Practical framework — PASS**  
   `Structure → Roles → Workflows → Information → Incentives → Norms → Networks → Decisions → Feedback` is explicitly a book synthesis, not a validated organizational taxonomy.

10. **Boundary with Chapters 28–30 — PASS**  
    Chapter 27 establishes the system map; later chapters address power/influence, interest conflict, and manipulation/defense.

11. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for organizational design, informal networks, routines, information flow, and incentives.

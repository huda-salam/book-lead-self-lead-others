# Evidence Packets v0.2 — Remaining Pre-Drafting Chapters

> This packet closes the second evidence pass for the 11 chapters previously marked as requiring dedicated source packets. It records what the evidence can support, what remains uncertain, and what the chapter must not claim.

## Ch 13 — Humor, Cletukan, dan Batas yang Aman
- Evidence: workplace humor is heterogeneous; a 2021 scoping review identifies humor styles, communication/discursive use, moderators, and cultural context as major dimensions. citeturn0search2turn0search4
- A meta-analysis of 49 studies (N=8,532) found associations between positive workplace humor and several employee/work outcomes, while explicitly noting construct and moderator limitations. citeturn0search0
- Recent organizational-humor review emphasizes both benefits and risks and calls attention to moderators such as culture, trust, communication, negotiation, and power. citeturn0search1turn0search7
- Chapter rule: humor is a contextual communication choice, not a personality virtue or universal tension-reduction formula.
- Practical heuristic: target → power difference → context → timing → audience → reversibility.
- Boundary: never imply that a joke is safe merely because the speaker intended it as humor.

## Ch 19 — Batas: Tegas Tanpa Menjadi Kasar
- Evidence: review literature on assertiveness reports mixed findings and proposes a curvilinear relationship between assertiveness and leadership effectiveness, with moderators and perception effects. citeturn1search15
- Chapter rule: teach assertiveness as calibrated expression of legitimate limits, not maximal directness.
- Practical model: state limit → relevant reason if useful → permissible alternative → consequence/next step.
- Boundary: wording cannot neutralize coercion, retaliation, or unsafe power asymmetry.
- Evidence status: sufficient for a bounded framework; avoid universal claims about “the right level” of assertiveness.

## Ch 23 — Mencari Akar Tanpa Terjebak Root Cause
- Evidence: causal inference in organizations is difficult because managers observe only parts of causal systems and hidden variables can bias assessments of action consequences. citeturn2search4
- A 2024 systematic review of strategic problem-solving methods identifies limitations in strategic root-cause analysis and argues that different problem situations may require different methods rather than one universal methodology. citeturn2search5
- Chapter rule: “root cause” is an intervention-oriented working question, not proof that one deepest cause exists.
- Practical model: outcome → candidate causes → evidence for/against → confounders → intervention point → uncertainty.
- Boundary: causal explanation requires stronger evidence than temporal sequence or plausible narrative.

## Ch 27 — Organisasi Bukan Hanya Struktur Resmi
- Evidence: a 2014 Academy of Management Annals review argues for integrating formal organization and informal social structure rather than treating them as separate literatures. citeturn1search10turn1search13
- A 2026 Oxford University Press synthesis treats formal and informal networks as intertwined systems that can reinforce or conflict with one another; informal structures can improve coordination but can also reinforce exclusion or dysfunction. citeturn1search0turn1search4
- Public-sector evidence indicates formal status and hierarchy shape information-search patterns in government organizations. citeturn1search14
- Chapter rule: teach network awareness without romanticizing informality or equating it with politics/manipulation.
- Practical model: formal owner → information source → affected party → blocker → influencer → authority.
- Boundary: an informal tie is not automatically illegitimate.

## Ch 30 — Manipulasi, Framing, dan Permainan Psikologis
- A classic meta-analysis of 206 documents and 24,483 judges found average real-time lie/truth discrimination around 54%, illustrating limits of unaided deception detection. citeturn2search9
- Organizational deception studies exist, but their estimates and contexts differ; this reinforces the need to avoid turning behavioral cues into certainty about intent. citeturn2search10
- Chapter rule: separate observable behavior, interpretation, incentive, missing information, and intent hypothesis.
- Practical model: behavior → evidence → missing information → incentives → alternative explanations → proportionate response.
- Boundary: “manipulatif” is a hypothesis requiring evidence, not a diagnosis triggered by discomfort or disagreement.

## Ch 34 — Memimpin Saat Tekanan Tinggi
- A 2023 Annual Review of Organizational Psychology and Organizational Behavior synthesizes crisis leadership research and identifies distinct competencies while emphasizing that crisis leadership remains an underdeveloped field. citeturn1search11
- A systematic interdisciplinary review examined 69 empirical articles and emphasizes that crises vary in nature, impact, and stakeholder perceptions. citeturn1search9
- Crisis-uncertainty research notes that acknowledging uncertainty is widely recommended, while empirical evidence on exactly how uncertainty communication works remains limited. citeturn1search1turn1search8
- Chapter rule: pressure leadership = stabilize → identify critical facts → define immediate objective → communicate uncertainty → assign ownership → review.
- Boundary: calm appearance does not prove good judgment; certainty in language should not exceed certainty in evidence.

## Ch 36 — Delegasi Bukan Memindahkan Beban
- Workplace meta-analysis of leader autonomy support covered 72 studies, 83 unique samples, and N=32,870; autonomy support was positively associated with autonomous motivation, well-being, and positive work behaviors. citeturn2search0turn2search3
- Research on delegation finds managers balance benefits of employee-specific knowledge and autonomy against loss-of-control/monitoring costs; delegation varies with task knowledge, exceptions, and monitoring feasibility. citeturn2search1
- Chapter rule: delegation transfers an outcome with sufficient authority, capability, constraints, and checkpoints; it is not task dumping.
- Practical model: outcome → authority → capability → constraints → checkpoint → feedback.
- Boundary: autonomy without capability/support can become abandonment; checkpoints are not automatically micromanagement.

## Ch 37 — Melihat Dua Langkah ke Depan
- Systems-thinking reviews show value in holistic approaches to complex decision situations and in combining multiple modelling/problem-structuring approaches. citeturn2search7turn2search8
- Organizational causal-inference work emphasizes that action consequences depend on partially observed causal systems. citeturn2search4
- Chapter rule: second-order reasoning is a structured exploration of possible downstream effects, not a prediction engine.
- Practical model: immediate effect → next effect → incentive change → dependency → adaptive response → reversibility.
- Boundary: do not present speculative chains as forecasts.

## Ch 38 — Strategi Berarti Memilih
- Choice research shows that trade-offs can produce different legitimate choice structures; there is no universal psychology of “one correct” trade-off response. citeturn1search3
- Resource-allocation research emphasizes heterogeneous routines and trade-offs in how organizations allocate scarce resources, with implications for adaptation and performance. citeturn2search13
- Chapter rule: strategy should be taught as choices and resource commitments under constraints, including what not to pursue.
- Practical model: objective → constraints → choices → trade-offs → resource commitment → stop/not-do list → review triggers.
- Boundary: strategy ≠ detailed planning; choice quality cannot be inferred solely from outcome.

## Ch 41 — Belajar dari Nabi dan Sahabat
- Source hierarchy must distinguish Qur'an, authenticated hadith, classical interpretation, historical/sirah reports, and modern interpretation.
- Qur'an 4:58 explicitly links entrusted responsibility with justice in judgment. citeturn3search4
- Sahih Muslim 55a presents sincerity/advice toward Allah, His Book, His Messenger, Muslim leaders, and the general community. citeturn3search0
- Sahih al-Bukhari 6114 defines strength in anger in terms of self-control, supporting the book's interest in response regulation rather than domination. citeturn3search8
- Sahih Muslim 1839a states obedience to rulers is bounded by the prohibition on obeying sinful commands; this is relevant when discussing authority, boundaries, and ethical obedience. citeturn3search11
- Chapter rule: extract behavior and context first; distinguish textual teaching from later interpretation; state transfer boundaries explicitly.
- Boundary: a narrative or scholarly interpretation must not be silently transformed into a universal workplace prescription.

## Ch 42 — Belajar dari Pemimpin, Tokoh Sejarah, Business Leaders, Profesional, dan Orang Biasa
- Evidence discipline: public success narratives are vulnerable to survivorship bias, halo effects, selection effects, and retrospective storytelling.
- Organizational research also cautions that causal conclusions require more than observed association or compelling narrative. citeturn2search4
- Chapter rule: the subject is not “hero worship” but case reasoning.
- Practical extraction: behavior → context → mechanism hypothesis → evidence → alternative explanation → boundary → transfer question.
- Boundary: success does not prove that a particular behavior caused success, and failure does not prove the opposite.

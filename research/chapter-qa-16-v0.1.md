# Chapter 16 QA — Trust, Psychological Safety, Accountability, dan Respect

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   The chapter treats relational conditions as leadership capability, not personality or culture slogans.

2. **Construct separation — PASS**  
   Trust, psychological safety, respect, and accountability are explicitly distinguished rather than collapsed into one construct.

3. **Psychological safety boundary — PASS**  
   Psychological safety is defined as interpersonal risk-taking safety, not comfort, agreement, absence of pressure, or absence of consequences.

4. **Trust boundary — PASS**  
   Trust is presented as calibrated and domain-specific rather than blind faith. Verification remains legitimate where risk requires it.

5. **Respect boundary — PASS**  
   Respect is separated from agreement and linked back to Chapter 11 without taking over its ownership.

6. **Accountability boundary — PASS**  
   Accountability is retained for commitments, standards, rules, and relevant consequences. Humiliation is explicitly separated from accountability.

7. **Boundary with Chapter 15 — PASS**  
   Chapter 15 owns feedback and difficult-conversation mechanics; Chapter 16 describes the relational conditions around them.

8. **Boundary with Chapters 17–20 — PASS**  
   Conflict, de-escalation, boundaries, and repair remain later chapters. Chapter 16 provides conditions rather than replacing those mechanisms.

9. **Mechanism — PASS**  
   The chapter explains how responses to bad news, correction, disagreement, and mistakes can shape the information environment while preserving structural explanations.

10. **Structural caution — PASS**  
    The chapter explicitly warns against psychologizing problems caused by unclear authority, workload, incentives, process, or information constraints.

11. **Practical framework — PASS**  
    `Respect → Safety to Speak → Trust Calibration → Accountability → Repair/Learning` is clearly labeled as a book synthesis, not a validated taxonomy or causal sequence.

12. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Claims about psychological safety and trust remain bounded to the cited literature; the book synthesis is not presented as empirically validated.

13. **Red-team — PASS WITH MINOR PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliographic verification and careful distinction between empirical findings and the book's integrative model.

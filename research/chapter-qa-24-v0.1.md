# Chapter 24 QA — Memilih Solusi dan Mengakui Trade-off

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Decision choice is framed as explicit prioritization under constraints, not as finding a universally best option.

2. **Boundary with Chapter 22 — PASS**  
   Chapter 22 owns alternative/hypothesis discipline; Chapter 24 owns comparative choice after alternatives are sufficiently developed.

3. **Boundary with Chapter 23 — PASS**  
   Chapter 23 owns causal explanation; Chapter 24 uses causal understanding as input to choice.

4. **Boundary with Chapter 25 — PASS**  
   Execution is deferred to Chapter 25.

5. **Boundary with Chapter 26 — PASS**  
   Outcome review is deferred to Chapter 26; Chapter 24 introduces review triggers without doing post-outcome evaluation.

6. **Mechanism — PASS**  
   Objective, constraints, criteria, alternatives, trade-offs, risk/uncertainty, choice, rationale, and review trigger are separated.

7. **Hard constraints versus preferences — PASS**  
   Legal, safety, integrity, contractual, and authority constraints are distinguished from preferences.

8. **False precision — PASS**  
   The chapter explicitly warns that scoring can create an illusion of objectivity when the underlying estimates are weak.

9. **Distributional effects — PASS**  
   The chapter asks who receives benefits and who bears cost/risk/workload.

10. **Uncertainty and reversibility — PASS**  
    The chapter connects decision depth with uncertainty and reversibility without presenting a rigid mathematical rule.

11. **Process versus outcome — PASS**  
    The chapter avoids judging decision quality solely from later outcome; Chapter 26 will own outcome review.

12. **Practical framework — PASS**  
    `Objective → Constraints → Criteria → Alternatives → Trade-offs → Risk/Uncertainty → Choice → Rationale → Review trigger` is explicitly a book synthesis, not a validated decision taxonomy.

13. **No universal winner — PASS**  
    Options are evaluated relative to objectives, constraints, and context rather than ranked universally.

14. **Red-team — PASS WITH MINOR PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires precise sourcing for decision analysis, MCDA, uncertainty, reversibility, and option-value claims.

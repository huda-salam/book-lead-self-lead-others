## 1. Structural QA

- Leader capability framing: **PASS**.
- Chapter has clear ownership: language as an instrument of leadership.
- Sequence is coherent: mechanism → distinctions → examples → working model → practice → boundary.
- Callouts are used as retrieval aids rather than evidence ratings: **PASS**.
- No structural dependency on later chapters that prevents standalone reading: **PASS**.

## 2. Boundary with Chapter 11

**Result: PASS.**

Chapter 11 owns respect and disagreement as relational/interactional capability. Chapter 12 operationalizes part of that capability through language choice.

The distinction should remain:
- Ch11: *bagaimana memperlakukan orang ketika setuju/tidak setuju*.
- Ch12: *bagaimana menyusun pesan agar substansi jelas tanpa menambah ancaman sosial yang tidak diperlukan*.

Do not let future revisions turn Ch12 into a second chapter on respect.

## 3. Boundary with Chapter 18

**Result: PASS WITH GUARDRAIL.**

Chapter 12 discusses lowering tension through wording and framing. Chapter 18 should own conflict de-escalation as a broader process involving interaction sequence, escalation dynamics, intervention, and safety.

Ch12 should not become a full conflict-management chapter.

Guardrail retained in the chapter:
> bahasa adalah instrumen, bukan pengganti diagnosis masalah.

## 4. Mechanism QA

**Result: PASS.**

Examples generally demonstrate a mechanism rather than merely offering “nicer phrases”:
- identity judgment → observable behavior;
- emotional heat → problem focus;
- ambiguity → explicit intent;
- absolute claim → calibrated evidence;
- hierarchy → awareness of power effects;
- politeness → clarity without camouflage.

This is important because the book trains leader mindset, not etiquette.

## 5. Firmness / hard-message QA

**Result: PASS.**

The chapter explicitly preserves:
- standards;
- accountability;
- refusal;
- warnings;
- risk escalation;
- safety instructions;
- decisions that must be delivered firmly.

The central distinction “turunkan suhu, jangan turunkan standar” is strong enough to carry this boundary.

## 6. Evidence / synthesis QA

**Result: PASS WITH CONDITIONS.**

The chapter clearly labels its working model as book synthesis and avoids claiming a universal formula.

Before publication:
1. replace internal research-result references with stable provenance records;
2. verify exact bibliographic details and DOI/URL for every cited study;
3. distinguish empirical findings from the book's practical synthesis;
4. retain uncertainty where evidence is context-dependent.

Important terms are used consistently:

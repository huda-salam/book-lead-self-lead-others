# Chapter 40 QA — Cara Membaca Sebuah Kasus

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Meta-reasoning framing — PASS**  
   The chapter teaches how to analyze cases rather than prescribing a single answer to cases.

2. **Narrative versus reality — PASS**  
   Case narratives are explicitly treated as selective representations rather than complete reality.

3. **Timeline and information-at-the-time — PASS**  
   The chapter protects against hindsight by reconstructing what was known at each decision point.

4. **Observation/interpretation separation — PASS**  
   Character labels are not accepted as facts without supporting evidence.

5. **Actor/context analysis — PASS**  
   Objectives, interests, authority, information, constraints, and incentives are considered without automatically excusing behavior.

6. **Decision versus outcome — PASS**  
   Decision quality and outcome quality are explicitly separated.

7. **Competing explanations — PASS**  
   The chapter avoids single-cause storytelling and links explanations to mechanisms and evidence.

8. **Counterfactual boundary — PASS**  
   Counterfactuals are treated as reasoning tools dependent on assumptions, not proof of what would certainly have happened.

9. **Transfer discipline — PASS**  
   Lessons should be expressed as mechanism plus boundary condition rather than copied surface behavior.

10. **Framework — PASS**  
    `Case → Timeline → Actors → Facts → Unknowns → Decision Points → Options → Mechanisms → Consequences → Attribution → Alternatives → Learning/Transfer` is explicitly a book synthesis, not a validated case-analysis taxonomy.

11. **Boundary with Chapters 21–26, 27–30, 41–43 — PASS**  
    Existing problem/decision and organizational reasoning tools remain source capabilities; Chapter 40 is the meta-reading method.

12. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for hindsight bias, attribution, causal reasoning, case-based learning, counterfactual reasoning, and transfer claims.

## Editorial verdict

**Proceed to Chapter 41 — Belajar dari Nabi dan Sahabat.**

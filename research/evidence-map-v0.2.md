# Evidence Map v0.2 — Communication, Conflict, Problem Solving, Leadership, Organization, Strategy, Practice

Tanggal riset: 20 September 2026

## 1. Ringkasan

Evidence pass kedua memperkuat beberapa keputusan foundation:

1. **Listening layak menjadi capability inti**, bukan sekadar etiket komunikasi. Meta-analysis 664 effect sizes dan 400.020 observasi menemukan perceived listening berasosiasi positif dengan work outcomes, dengan hubungan lebih kuat terhadap relationship quality daripada performance. [1]
2. **Conflict tidak boleh diajarkan sebagai sesuatu yang otomatis produktif.** Meta-analysis 2026 yang diperbarui menemukan task, relationship, process, dan status conflict semuanya memiliki hubungan negatif dengan team performance secara rata-rata, meskipun task dan relationship conflict menunjukkan heterogenitas lintas konteks. [2]
3. **Feedback harus diperlakukan sebagai proses**, bukan sekadar "memberi kritik". Meta-analysis 2026 terhadap 24 studi, 595.950 partisipan, dan 75 effect sizes menunjukkan karakteristik feedback berhubungan dengan tahapan pemrosesan feedback seperti perception, acceptance, desire to respond, dan intended response; credibility merupakan karakteristik yang paling sering diteliti. [3]
4. **Psychological safety perlu ditempatkan bersama accountability, learning, dan relationship quality**, bukan sebagai sinonim kenyamanan. Evidence meta-analitiknya kuat, tetapi construct dan konteks tetap penting. [4]
5. **Conflict management harus dipandang sebagai dynamic process.** Literatur review menunjukkan conflict dapat berubah bentuk dari waktu ke waktu dan berinteraksi dengan psychological safety, conflict management, dan konteks organisasi. [5]
6. **Problem solving dan strategic thinking perlu diposisikan sebagai reasoning disciplines**, bukan kumpulan slogan atau "trik berpikir".
7. **Practice system** perlu dibangun dari prinsip learning/behavior change dan diuji; jangan mengasumsikan bahwa membaca insight otomatis mengubah perilaku.

## 3. Communication

### Evidence interpretation

The strongest direct evidence located in this pass concerns perceived listening. The 2024 Journal of Business and Psychology meta-analysis reports a positive association with work outcomes (mean observed r=.39; mean corrected rho=.44), with relationship quality stronger than performance. [1]

### Book implications

Listening should be taught as a sequence of observable behaviors:

**Attend → Clarify → Reflect → Test understanding → Separate experience from interpretation → Respond**

The book should distinguish:

- listening from waiting to speak;
- understanding from agreement;
- validation from endorsement;
- empathy from emotional contagion;
- politeness from effectiveness.

### Practical consequence

For the user's specific concern about "ngompori" when someone vents, the book should teach a three-way distinction:

**Acknowledge the experience → test the interpretation → avoid automatically amplifying the conclusion.**

Example principle, not final book copy:

> "Saya paham kenapa itu membuatmu kesal" does not mean "berarti mereka memang salah."

This distinction should become a canonical communication concept.

## 4. Feedback

The 2026 systematic review/meta-analysis on supervisory feedback examined 24 peer-reviewed studies, N=595,950, 75 effect sizes, and 26 feedback characteristics. It emphasizes that feedback effectiveness is not uniform and that employee processing includes multiple stages. [3]

### Book implications

The book should not teach feedback as:

**"Beritahu orang apa salahnya."**

Instead, feedback should consider:

1. credibility of source;
2. clarity of observation;
3. relevance to task/goal;
4. interpretation;
5. recipient's processing;
6. opportunity to respond;
7. actionability;
8. follow-up.

This also supports the broader thesis that **communication quality is partly determined by what happens after the words are spoken**.

## 5. Conflict

The updated 2026 meta-analysis is particularly important for this book. It synthesizes task, relationship, process, and status conflict and reports negative average relationships with team performance for all four dimensions. It also finds substantial heterogeneity for task and relationship conflict and identifies national culture, team features, and methodological factors as moderators. [2]

### Book implication

We should abandon the simplistic educational trope:

> "Conflict is good because different opinions produce innovation."

A more defensible framing is:

> **Perbedaan pendapat dapat menjadi bahan pengambilan keputusan; conflict is a social process with costs and context, not an automatic source of performance.**

The book therefore needs to teach:

- disagreement without personal attack;
- early detection of escalation;
- separating task disagreement from relationship threat;
- process conflict;
- status/ego conflict;
- de-escalation;
- when to continue discussion;
- when to pause;
- when to establish boundaries;
- when to escalate.

Conflict becomes an important bridge between **Part II (choose response)** and **Part III (communication/relationship)**.

## 6. Psychological safety, trust, and accountability

Psychological safety has strong meta-analytic evidence, including 136 independent samples, >22,000 individuals, and nearly 5,000 groups. [4]

But the book should not collapse these concepts:

- **Psychological safety:** perceived interpersonal safety for speaking/interacting.
- **Trust:** expectation about another party's reliability, intentions, or behavior.
- **Accountability:** expectation that people remain answerable for actions/results.
- **Respect:** treating people as persons whose dignity and legitimate interests matter.

### Core proposition for the book

A healthy work relationship can require all four simultaneously:

**safe enough to speak + clear enough to disagree + accountable enough to perform + respectful enough to preserve dignity.**

This should become a conceptual test used throughout communication, leadership, and management chapters.

## 7. Problem solving

The evidence pass does not justify selecting a single universal problem-solving framework yet.

The book should instead establish a reasoning discipline:

**Signal → Problem definition → Evidence → Causes/constraints → Options → Trade-offs → Decision → Action → Feedback**

Important distinction:

- symptom is not necessarily problem;
- explanation is not necessarily cause;
- plausible cause is not established cause;
- option is not decision;
- decision is not execution;
- outcome is not proof of reasoning quality.

### Anti-patterns to explicitly teach

- solution-first thinking;
- problem inflation;
- blaming a person when a process/system explains more;
- confusing urgency with importance;
- treating anecdotes as sufficient evidence;
- optimizing one metric while damaging the system;
- escalating before clarifying.

## 8. Decision making

Decision quality should be separated from outcome quality.

A reasonable decision can produce a bad outcome because of uncertainty. A poor decision can occasionally produce a good outcome.

Therefore the book should teach readers to review:

**What did I know then? What did I assume? What alternatives did I consider? What risks were visible? What was reversible? What information was missing?**

This is more useful than judging every decision solely from hindsight.

## 9. Leadership

Leadership should be framed as a combination of:

- direction;
- responsibility;
- credibility;
- influence;
- coordination;
- judgment;
- accountability;
- development of others.

The book should avoid the claim that "anyone can be a leader" if interpreted as "everyone has equal organizational authority." A more precise proposition is:

> **Leadership behaviors can be practiced without formal authority, while formal leadership also carries responsibilities and powers that informal influence does not.**

This distinction is important for readers who are staff rather than managers.

## 10. Organizational influence and politics

This domain remains intentionally defensive.

The book may teach readers to recognize:

- competing interests;
- formal vs informal power;
- coalition formation;
- agenda setting;
- information asymmetry;
- framing;
- reputation and credibility;
- resistance;
- incentives;
- organizational narratives.

It should not teach:

- deception as a default strategy;
- exploiting vulnerability;
- manufacturing conflict;
- coercive manipulation;
- political retaliation.

### Core ethical proposition

**Memahami permainan organisasi tidak berarti harus bermain secara kotor.**

A reader should become harder to manipulate, not better at manipulating others.

---

## 11. Strategic thinking

Strategic thinking will be defined operationally rather than as "thinking long term."

Candidate elements:

1. define desired outcome;
2. understand system/context;
3. identify constraints;
4. distinguish signal from noise;
5. identify important dependencies;
6. consider second-order effects;
7. compare trade-offs;
8. preserve optionality where useful;
9. choose what not to do;
10. revisit assumptions as conditions change.

This domain should connect closely to problem solving and decision making, not become a detached strategy textbook.

---

## 12. Practice and behavioral change

This remains the least-developed evidence area in the current foundation.

The book should eventually use a repeated micro-format:

**Situation → Automatic response → Why it fails → Read the situation → Principle → Response options → Example language → Risks → Practice**

But this is a **candidate pedagogical format**, not yet an evidence-validated intervention.

The next research pass should identify evidence for:

- deliberate practice;
- implementation intentions;
- behavioral rehearsal;
- reflection;
- feedback loops;
- habit formation;
- scenario-based learning.

The book should also avoid fake precision such as assigning a "behavior improvement score" without validated measurement.

---

## 13. Major foundation adjustment

Evidence pass kedua mengubah satu hal penting:

### Previous tendency

Conflict was implicitly treated as something to be "managed well."

### Revised model

Conflict is treated as a **cost-bearing social process** that may need:

- prevention,
- clarification,
- containment,
- transformation,
- negotiation,
- boundary setting,
- or escalation.

This is more consistent with the updated evidence and prevents the book from romanticizing disagreement.

# Chapter 39 QA — Mengubah Rencana Ketika Dunia Berubah

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Strategic adaptation framing — PASS**  
   Adaptation is framed as evidence- and condition-sensitive adjustment, not impulsive change.

2. **Goal/strategy/method/execution separation — PASS**  
   The chapter provides a clear diagnostic ladder so a local execution failure does not automatically trigger a strategic pivot.

3. **Persistence/adaptation distinction — PASS**  
   Persistence is retained as pursuit of a valid goal while methods can change.

4. **Material change threshold — PASS**  
   The chapter distinguishes meaningful changes from normal environmental noise and avoids treating every setback as a pivot signal.

5. **Preserve the core — PASS**  
   Adaptation explicitly asks what remains valid before changing direction.

6. **Evidence-triggered change — PASS**  
   Review triggers, indicators, experiments, and change logs support traceable adaptation.

7. **Anti-sunk-cost reasoning — PASS**  
   Past investment is distinguished from future decision value.

8. **Communication — PASS**  
   Changes are explained through what changed, what remains, what changes, why, and when review occurs.

9. **Strategic drift — PASS**  
   The chapter recognizes excessive persistence as a potential failure mode while avoiding automatic claims that declining outcomes prove strategic failure.

10. **Learning loop — PASS**  
    `Plan → Execute → Observe → Compare → Update` is contrasted with defend/rationalize behavior without psychologizing individuals.

11. **Framework — PASS**  
    `Observe → Compare → Diagnose Change → Preserve Core → Adjust → Communicate → Test → Learn` is explicitly a book synthesis, not a validated adaptation taxonomy.

12. **Boundary with Chapters 4, 26, 37, 38, 43 — PASS**  
    Resilience/persistence, review, second-order thinking, strategic choice, and failure learning retain distinct ownership.

13. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for persistence/disengagement, organizational adaptation, dynamic self-regulation, experimentation, and strategic change claims.

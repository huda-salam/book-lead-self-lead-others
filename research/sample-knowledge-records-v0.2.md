# Sample Knowledge Records v0.2 — Discipline, Consistency, Persistence

## Concept: SELF.DISCIPLINE

- knowledge_type: concept
- title: Disiplin Diri
- canonical_owner: SELF.DISCIPLINE
- summary: kemampuan menjalankan standar atau perilaku yang telah dipilih meski motivasi, kenyamanan, atau perhatian berubah.
- scope: execution of chosen behavior under ordinary friction and distraction
- boundary: not punishment, rigidity, productivity obsession, or proof of moral worth
- related_concepts: SELF.RESPONSE, SELF.CONSISTENCY, SELF.RESILIENCE, PRACTICE.OS
- evidence_status: theoretical
- application_context: personal routines, response control, follow-through
- distinction: discipline is a capability/process; consistency is the repeated pattern observed over time.

## Concept: SELF.CONSISTENCY

- knowledge_type: concept
- title: Konsistensi
- canonical_owner: SELF.CONSISTENCY
- summary: pola menjalankan perilaku atau standar secara berulang dan cukup stabil terhadap tujuan yang dipilih.
- scope: repeated execution over time
- boundary: consistency does not require identical methods; context and sustainability matter
- related_concepts: SELF.DISCIPLINE, SELF.PERSISTENCE, STRAT.ADAPTATION
- evidence_status: theoretical
- application_context: habit maintenance, project execution, leadership reliability

## Concept: SELF.PERSISTENCE

- knowledge_type: concept
- title: Persistensi
- canonical_owner: SELF.PERSISTENCE
- summary: keberlanjutan mengejar tujuan yang valid melewati hambatan sambil tetap terbuka terhadap perubahan metode.
- scope: goal pursuit under setbacks
- boundary: not stubborn repetition, sunk-cost commitment, or refusal to stop when the goal loses validity
- related_concepts: SELF.RESILIENCE, SELF.DISCIPLINE, DECISION.GOAL_DISCIPLINE, STRAT.ADAPTATION
- evidence_status: theoretical
- application_context: long projects, difficult behavior change, organizational initiatives

## Concept: DECISION.GOAL_DISCIPLINE

- knowledge_type: concept
- title: Disiplin terhadap Tujuan
- canonical_owner: DECISION.GOAL_DISCIPLINE

- scope: decisions and problem solving
- boundary: does not require indefinite pursuit of an invalid or obsolete goal

- application_context: project failure, solution revision, strategic adaptation

## Concept: LITERACY.DISCIPLINE

- knowledge_type: concept
- title: Disiplin Literasi
- canonical_owner: LITERACY.DISCIPLINE

- evidence_status: practice_hypothesis

## Practice: PRACTICE.CONSISTENCY_RECOVERY

- knowledge_type: practice
- title: Recovery Saat Konsistensi Turun
- canonical_owner: PRACTICE.OS
- practice_goal: kembali ke jalur setelah penurunan eksekusi tanpa menunggu motivasi pulih sepenuhnya
- trigger: menyadari pola “ngotot di awal → lemas di tengah → mulai menghindar”
- steps:
  1. name the current state without self-attack
  2. check whether the goal is still valid
  3. identify the smallest meaningful next action
  4. remove one major friction
  5. execute
  6. set a near-term checkpoint
  7. review whether the method or commitment needs adjustment
- failure_modes: restarting too ambitiously; changing goals merely to escape discomfort; continuing a failed method out of ego; turning review into self-blame
- review_question: “Apakah saya sedang mempertahankan tujuan, atau sekadar mempertahankan cara yang sudah saya pilih?”
- frequency: triggered plus scheduled review
- related_concepts: SELF.DISCIPLINE, SELF.CONSISTENCY, SELF.PERSISTENCE, DECISION.GOAL_DISCIPLINE, PRACTICE.OS
- evidence_status: practice_hypothesis

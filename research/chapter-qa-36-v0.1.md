# Chapter 36 QA — Delegasi Bukan Memindahkan Beban

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Management capability framing — PASS**  
   Delegation is framed as allocating work and appropriate authority, not merely offloading tasks.

2. **Outcome versus activity — PASS**  
   Delegated work begins with outcome, context, authority, and boundaries rather than a bare task instruction.

3. **Responsibility/authority alignment — PASS**  
   The chapter explicitly identifies responsibility without sufficient authority as a delegation failure mode.

4. **Capability/stakes fit — PASS**  
   Delegation level is conditioned on capability, information, workload, authority, risk, and reversibility.

5. **Micromanagement versus abandonment — PASS**  
   Checkpoints are distinguished from excessive control, while abandonment is identified as the opposite failure mode.

6. **Resource adequacy — PASS**  
   Access, data, time, budget, authority, and support are treated as conditions of meaningful delegation.

7. **Accountability — PASS**  
   Delegation does not erase the leader's management responsibility or formal obligations.

8. **Development boundary — PASS**  
   Delegation can develop people but is not reduced to a development program; Chapter 33 retains development ownership.

9. **Trust/control distinction — PASS**  
   Calibrated trust does not replace appropriate control, and control does not require micromanagement.

10. **Framework — PASS**  
    `Select → Define Outcome → Assign Authority → Set Guardrails → Resource → Checkpoint → Support → Review → Adjust` is explicitly a book synthesis, not a validated delegation taxonomy.

11. **Failure diagnosis — PASS**  
    The chapter prevents premature people-blame by checking clarity, authority, resources, dependencies, capability, workload, deadline, and direction changes.

12. **Boundary with Chapters 25, 31, 33, 34 — PASS**  
    Execution, credibility/ownership, development, and pressure remain distinct.

13. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for delegation, empowerment, autonomy, management control, and developmental assignments.

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Repair is treated as an active leadership capability involving ownership, correction, prevention, and rebuilding predictability.

2. **Boundary with Chapter 16 — PASS**  
   Chapter 16 owns trust, psychological safety, accountability, and respect as relational conditions. Chapter 20 focuses on post-rupture repair.

3. **Boundary with Chapter 17 — PASS**  
   Conflict diagnosis remains Chapter 17; repair begins after an event/rupture has occurred.

4. **Boundary with Chapter 18 — PASS**  
   De-escalation remains Chapter 18; repair is not merely making the conversation calmer.

5. **Boundary with Chapter 19 — PASS**  
   Boundary and accountability remain available when repair is insufficient or inappropriate.

6. **Mechanism — PASS**  
   `Acknowledge → Own → Repair → Prevent → Rebuild → Review` gives the chapter a concrete operating sequence.

7. **Impact versus intention — PASS**  
   The chapter avoids both intent erasure and intent-as-excuse. It recommends acknowledging impact while accurately describing intention.

8. **Accountability — PASS**  
   Ownership is bounded to actual responsibility; the chapter explicitly rejects taking total blame for multi-party events.

9. **Trust repair — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
   The chapter correctly treats trust rebuilding as requiring subsequent evidence/behavior rather than persuasion alone. Final publication requires precise sourcing of apology/trust-repair literature.

10. **Forgiveness/reconciliation agency — PASS**  
    The injured party is not required to forgive immediately or restore the previous relationship.

11. **Public repair — PASS**  
    The chapter appropriately notes that a public mistake may require proportionate public correction without turning repair into performance.

12. **Serious misconduct boundary — PASS**  
    Safety, violence, harassment, discrimination, fraud, threats, legal violations, and serious abuse of authority are not reduced to interpersonal repair.

13. **Practical framework — PASS**  
    The framework is explicitly labeled as a book synthesis, not a validated repair protocol.

14. **Red-team — PASS WITH MINOR PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification and careful separation of interpersonal apology findings from organizational trust-repair evidence.

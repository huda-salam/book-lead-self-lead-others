Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Communication is framed as audience-sensitive leadership capability, not personality performance.

2. **Boundary with Chapter 10 — PASS**  
   Listening remains owned by Chapter 10; this chapter focuses on adapting message delivery and responsibility to audience.

3. **Boundary with Chapter 11 — PASS**  
   Respect/disagreement remain Chapter 11's core; Chapter 14 operationalizes communication across hierarchical audiences.

4. **Boundary with Chapter 12 — PASS**  
   General tension-reducing language remains Chapter 12; Chapter 14 focuses on audience, objective, authority, risk, information, medium, and confirmation.

5. **Boundary with Chapter 15 — PASS**  
   Feedback and difficult conversations remain Chapter 15. Chapter 14 only uses correction as an audience example.

6. **Mechanism — PASS WITH REVISIONS**  
   The chapter distinguishes content from delivery and shows how authority, information asymmetry, risk, and relationship alter communication requirements without changing factual standards.

7. **Hierarchy/evidence posture — PASS WITH CONDITIONS**  
   Evidence supports the claim that hierarchy, power, status, and context can affect voice and communication. Findings are not generalized into universal rules about every superior, peer, or subordinate.

8. **Power/status distinction — PASS**  
   The chapter does not treat hierarchy as proof of competence or truth.

9. **Practical framework — PASS**  
   The Audience → Objective → Authority → Risk → Information → Delivery → Confirmation framework is explicitly labeled as a book synthesis, not a validated taxonomy.

10. **Political/organizational manipulation boundary — PASS**  
    The chapter distinguishes adaptive communication from hiding facts, inconsistent messaging for personal advantage, or using hierarchy to suppress relevant information.

11. **Anti-analysis-paralysis — PASS**  
    The framework is presented as a lightweight pre-communication check, not a requirement to over-engineer ordinary conversations.

12. **Red-team — PASS WITH MINOR PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication still needs bibliographic/provenance verification for the cited studies and careful preservation of study-design limitations.

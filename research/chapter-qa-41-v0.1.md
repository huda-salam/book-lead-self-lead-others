Result: blocked.

### Source conflation
Risk: treating Qur'an, hadith, and historical reports as equal.
Result: blocked by source hierarchy.

Risk: treating Abu Bakr/Umar examples as identical in authority to Prophetic example.
Result: blocked.

Risk: reader uses a story to legitimize an already chosen action.
Result: addressed through confirmation-bias warning and source/context loop.

# Chapter 35 QA — Tidak Semua Hal Harus Dikerjakan

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Management capability framing — PASS**  
   Prioritization is framed as allocation of scarce attention, time, people, and resources, not merely task ordering.

2. **Outcome versus activity — PASS**  
   The chapter distinguishes activity volume from movement toward outcomes.

3. **Urgency versus importance — PASS**  
   The distinction is presented as a practical reasoning aid, not as a universal validated taxonomy.

4. **Trade-offs — PASS**  
   Every meaningful priority choice is connected to what will be delayed, reduced, delegated, or stopped.

5. **Resource alignment — PASS**  
   The chapter explicitly states that a priority should affect allocation, sequencing, staffing, calendar, authority, or dependencies.

6. **Priority churn — PASS**  
   Frequent unexplained changes are identified as a coordination risk while preserving the legitimacy of changing priorities when conditions change.

7. **Not-now and stop decisions — PASS**  
   The chapter treats postponement and stopping as legitimate management decisions rather than failure.

8. **Scoring caution — PASS**  
   Unsupported precision is avoided; the Priority Board is explicitly an aid to make reasoning visible, not an objective scoring machine.

9. **Governance boundary — PASS**  
   Prioritization cannot override legal, safety, compliance, or authority obligations.

10. **Framework — PASS**  
    `Objective → Constraints → Consequences → Prioritize → Allocate → Communicate → Review` is explicitly a book synthesis, not a validated prioritization taxonomy.

11. **Boundary with Chapters 24, 25, 36–38 — PASS**  
    Decision trade-offs, execution, delegation, second-order thinking, and strategic choice retain distinct ownership.

12. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for multiple-goal pursuit, attention/priority switching, resource allocation, and opportunity-cost claims.

## Editorial verdict

**Proceed to Chapter 36 — Delegasi Bukan Memindahkan Beban.**

# Chapter 34 QA — Memimpin Saat Tekanan Tinggi

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Pressure leadership is framed as maintaining judgment, coordination, communication, and proportionate action under constrained conditions.

2. **Pressure is contextual — PASS**  
   The chapter avoids treating pressure as an automatic cause of irrationality or incompetence and preserves differences in experience, task, resources, and context.

3. **Stabilization before optimization — PASS**  
   The chapter prioritizes containment of critical harm before optimization.

4. **Prioritization — PASS**  
   “Critical now / important but deferrable / useful but noncritical / stop” is explicitly presented as a practical aid, not a validated taxonomy.

5. **Known/unknown/assumption — PASS**  
   The chapter preserves the epistemic discipline established in Chapters 7 and 23.

6. **Calm versus passivity — PASS**  
   Calmness is not equated with softness, silence, or lack of decisive action.

7. **Escalation — PASS**  
   Escalation is framed as responsible when authority, resources, capacity, or risk thresholds require it.

8. **Communication — PASS**  
   High-pressure communication emphasizes event, priority, ownership, unknowns, and next update without requiring false certainty.

9. **Recovery — PASS**  
   Recovery is included as part of sustainable pressure leadership rather than as an optional afterthought.

10. **Hindsight discipline — PASS**  
    Post-pressure review is linked to Chapter 26 and separates outcome, decision, information available at the time, execution, and context.

11. **Framework — PASS**  
    `Stabilize → Prioritize → Clarify → Decide → Coordinate → Monitor → Escalate/Adapt → Recover → Review` is explicitly a book synthesis, not a validated crisis-leadership taxonomy or intervention protocol.

12. **Boundary with adjacent chapters — PASS**  
    Chapters 6, 9, 16, 25, and 26 retain their respective ownership.

13. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for stress/decision-making, crisis leadership, information sharing, recovery, and after-action review claims.

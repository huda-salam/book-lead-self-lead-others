## 1. Kontrak matriks

Setiap bab dipetakan melalui:
Capability → Canonical Concepts → Core Claims → Evidence Requirement → Cases → Questions → Red Flags → Decisions → Practice → Dependencies → Boundaries → Status.

Kode status:
- EVIDENCE-MAPPED — klaim utama sudah memiliki arah evidence yang memadai.
- PARTIAL — sebagian klaim sudah didukung; masih perlu pencarian/penilaian evidence.
- RESEARCH REQUIRED — jangan membuat klaim kuat sebelum riset tambahan.
- CASE-HEAVY — validitas terutama bergantung pada pemilihan kasus dan disiplin interpretasi.
- PRACTICE-HEAVY — evidence mendukung mekanisme/latihan, tetapi outcome dunia nyata harus dinyatakan sebagai tujuan latihan, bukan jaminan.

## 3. Evidence backbone saat ini

### Sudah relatif kuat
- Listening: meta-analysis Kluger et al. 2024; evidence links perceived listening with work outcomes, with stronger association for relationship quality than performance.
- Psychological safety: Frazier et al. 2017 meta-analysis.
- Conflict: 2026 meta-analysis already recorded in evidence-map-v0.2.
- Feedback: 2026 systematic review/meta-analysis of 24 studies, N=595,950, 75 effect sizes across 26 feedback characteristics.
- Humility: evidence-map-v0.3 includes meta-analytic and construct-clarification evidence; claims remain bounded because construct overlap and measurement limitations remain.
- Perspective-taking: evidence-map-v0.3 records context-dependent effects; do not present perspective-taking as universally beneficial.
- Organizational politics/power: reviews distinguish political characteristics/actions/outcomes; power can arise through different forms of capital and mechanisms including status, identification, legitimacy, and resource dependence.
- Hindsight: meta-analysis supports hindsight bias under some conditions, with moderators; do not overstate magnitude.
- Implementation intentions: meta-analytic evidence supports if-then planning; later work across 642 tests shows effects vary by outcome, motivation, format, and rehearsal.

## 4. AI/retrieval compatibility

Final chapter records should expose:
chapter_id; canonical_concepts; questions; cases; red_flags; decisions; practice; depends_on; used_by; evidence_refs; boundaries; status.

Retrieval route:
Entry → Canonical owner → Mechanism → Evidence → Boundary → Application → Practice → Review

AI answer discipline:
- Book says — canonical synthesis.
- Evidence shows — sourced empirical claim.
- Inference — reasoning from evidence/context.
- Recommendation/Practice — action proposal.
- Unknown — evidence or context insufficient.

A case may illustrate or generate a hypothesis; it does not become causal evidence merely because the story is compelling.

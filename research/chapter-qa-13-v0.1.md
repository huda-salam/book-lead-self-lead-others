2. **Boundary with Chapter 11 (Respect) — PASS**  
   Respect remains the broader relational principle; this chapter owns humor as a special communicative act with ambiguity and social risk.

3. **Boundary with Chapter 12 (Language) — PASS**

4. **Boundary with Chapter 16 (Psychological Safety) — PASS**

5. **Mechanism — PASS AFTER REVISION**

6. **Power and laughter — PASS AFTER REVISION**  
   Wording now avoids assuming that subordinate laughter is coerced. It states that unequal relationships can make acceptance signals harder to interpret.

7. **Cultural/context boundary — PASS AFTER REVISION**  
   Chapter explicitly notes that humor standards vary by culture, relationship, generation, team, and workplace context.

8. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**

10. **Firmness and boundaries — PASS**

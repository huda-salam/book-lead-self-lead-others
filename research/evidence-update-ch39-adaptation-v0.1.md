# Evidence Update — Chapter 39: Mengubah Rencana Ketika Dunia Berubah

## Evidence focus

Chapter 39 treats adaptation as the capability to update plans, methods, priorities, or strategy when material evidence, assumptions, constraints, or environmental conditions change—without confusing adaptation with impulsive change.

### Core evidence posture

- Plans are based on assumptions and conditions that can change.
- Adaptation can be necessary when evidence invalidates important assumptions or when constraints/environment materially shift.
- Persistence and adaptation are complementary: a valid goal may require a different method.
- Constant switching can destroy learning, coordination, and strategic coherence.
- Good adaptation requires identifying what changed, what remains valid, and what should change.
- Learning requires feedback and comparison between expectations and observed outcomes.
- Adaptive organizations often need both exploration and exploitation; excessive focus on either can create problems.
- Small experiments can reduce uncertainty where stakes and reversibility permit.
- Adaptation should preserve legitimate constraints, values, obligations, and strategic intent unless those too are explicitly reconsidered.
- A failed outcome is not by itself proof that the underlying goal or strategy was wrong.

## Editorial implications

1. Name the original assumption.
2. Identify the new evidence or constraint.
3. Separate goal, strategy, method, and execution.
4. Decide what should remain stable.
5. Change the smallest level that solves the changed condition where possible.
6. Communicate why the change is being made.
7. Avoid defending sunk costs merely because a plan has already been announced.
8. Avoid changing direction merely because progress is uncomfortable.
9. Use experiments and review triggers when uncertainty is high.
10. Capture learning so the organization does not repeat the same reasoning.

## Proposed book synthesis

**Observe → Compare → Diagnose Change → Preserve Core → Adjust → Communicate → Test → Learn**

This is a book working model, not a validated adaptation taxonomy.

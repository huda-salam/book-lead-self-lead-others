# Chapter 30 QA — Manipulasi, Framing, dan Permainan Psikologis

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Chapter teaches defensive literacy and preservation of agency, not manipulation tactics.

2. **Framing versus manipulation — PASS**  
   Ordinary framing is distinguished from materially misleading omission, deception, coercion, and exploitation.

3. **Evidence over motive — PASS**  
   The chapter repeatedly converts suspected motives into observable claims, evidence gaps, pressure signals, and verification steps.

4. **Psychological-label caution — PASS**  
   “Gaslighting,” “toxic,” and “manipulative” are not used as diagnoses from isolated incidents; observable patterns are preferred.

5. **Power integration — PASS**  
   Authority, status, information, and dependency connect the chapter to Chapter 28 without duplicating its power map.

6. **Interest-conflict boundary — PASS**  
   Chapter 29 owns substantive interest conflict; Chapter 30 addresses problematic influence during such situations.

7. **Defensive framework — PASS**  
   `Signal → Claim → Evidence → Context → Pressure → Alternative → Boundary → Verify → Decide/Escalate` is explicitly a book defensive synthesis, not a validated manipulation-detection taxonomy.

8. **Agency — PASS**  
   Pause, verification, alternatives, boundaries, documentation, consultation, and escalation are emphasized as agency-preserving actions.

9. **Ethical boundary — PASS**  
   The chapter explicitly rejects retaliation and manipulation in response to manipulation.

10. **Formal-mechanism boundary — PASS**  
    Serious misconduct, threats, fraud, harassment, safety risks, and abuse of authority are not treated as communication problems alone.

11. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for framing, social influence, information asymmetry, pressure, judgment under uncertainty, and cautious use of psychological-manipulation literature.

## Editorial verdict

**PASS. Part VI complete. Proceed to Part VII, Chapter 31.**

# Chapter 38 QA — Strategi Berarti Memilih

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Strategic capability framing — PASS**  
   Strategy is framed as consequential choices about direction, activity, capability, positioning, and resource allocation rather than aspirations or task lists.

2. **Diagnosis before prescription — PASS**  
   The chapter starts strategic reasoning with diagnosis and distinguishes observation from a provisional diagnosis.

3. **Objective versus strategy — PASS**  
   Desired outcomes are distinguished from the choices used to pursue them.

4. **Trade-offs and exclusion — PASS**  
   The chapter explicitly requires what-not-to-pursue decisions and acknowledges opportunity costs.

5. **Resource commitment — PASS**  
   Strategy is tested by whether it changes resource, capability, process, incentive, technology, attention, or leadership allocation.

6. **Strategic coherence — PASS**  
   Potential tensions between choices and incompatible capability requirements are surfaced.

7. **Uncertainty and assumptions — PASS**  
   Strategy is not presented as prediction; material assumptions and indicators are made explicit.

8. **Theory of action — PASS**  
   Strategic claims are connected to a mechanism rather than left as slogans.

9. **Strategy versus plan/priority — PASS**  
   The chapter preserves distinctions between strategic direction, shorter-term priorities, and execution plans.

10. **Adaptation boundary — PASS**  
    Consistency is distinguished from rigidity; Chapter 39 retains ownership of adaptation.

11. **Framework — PASS**  
    `Diagnosis → Objective → Choice Set → Trade-offs → Coherence → Resource Commitment → Execution Logic → Assumptions → Review/Adapt` is explicitly a book synthesis, not a validated strategy taxonomy.

12. **Boundary with Chapters 24, 25, 35, 37, 39 — PASS**  
    Decision trade-offs, execution, operational priority, second-order reasoning, and adaptation remain distinct.

13. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for strategy-as-choice, positioning, activity systems, capabilities, resource allocation, and adaptive strategy claims.

## Editorial verdict

**Proceed to Chapter 39 — Mengubah Rencana Ketika Dunia Berubah.**

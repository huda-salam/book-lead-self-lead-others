## 2. Evidence QA — PASS WITH REVISIONS

The evidence supports cautious claims.

A meta-analysis of dispositional optimism and coping found associations with approach coping and lower avoidance coping, while noting moderators and stronger relationships in English-speaking samples. citeturn0search12

The literature on unrealistic optimism shows that people can make overly favorable predictions and that consequences for decision making and behavior are context-dependent. citeturn0search8turn0search6

### Required evidence boundary

Do not imply that “realistic optimism” is a single empirically settled construct with a universally optimal level.

The chapter's **positive realism** is a book construct/synthesis informed by several literatures.

Use explicit language such as:

> “Dalam buku ini, positive realism digunakan sebagai…”

This keeps canonical ownership clear.

## 3. Mechanism QA — PASS

Mechanism:

**state of affairs → distinguish known/unknown/changeable → preserve constructive orientation → evaluate options → act → feedback → update**

This is coherent with the book's larger self-regulation and literacy architecture.

## 4. “Good / Bad / Unknown / Changeable” QA

Strong pedagogical device.

Boundary:
- categories can overlap;
- “changeable” is context-dependent;
- something may be influenceable but not fully controllable.

Recommended wording:

> “Masukkan ke kategori yang paling membantu untuk berpikir saat ini; kategori ini bukan klasifikasi ontologis yang kaku.”

## 5. Risk of over-rationalization

The chapter may become too cognitive if emotion is treated as irrelevant.

Current text correctly preserves emotion.

Keep the Chapter 2 link:
- emotion can inform;
- emotion does not determine the prediction.

## 6. Risk of excessive caution

The chapter must not become:

> “Jangan pernah yakin.”

The intended principle is **calibrated confidence**, not permanent doubt.

Add or preserve:

> “Ketidakpastian yang jujur tidak mengharuskan kita menunda semua tindakan.”

## 8. Retrieval QA

### Questions
- “Bagaimana tetap positif tanpa menyangkal masalah?”

- “Bagaimana tetap punya harapan ketika evidence buruk?”
- “Kapan optimisme berubah menjadi denial?”

### Cases
- proyek terlambat;
- target tidak tercapai;
- orang lain berkata “tetap positif”;
- data buruk tetapi masih ada opsi;
- peluang kecil dengan biaya eksperimen rendah.

### Red flags

- “Pasti gagal.”
- “Jangan negatif.”
- “Tidak ada kabar buruk berarti aman.”
- “Saya realistis” sebagai alasan tidak pernah mencoba.

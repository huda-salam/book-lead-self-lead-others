## Cross-cutting canonical concepts

- SELF.DISCIPLINE: execute chosen standards despite ordinary friction; not moral worth.
- SELF.CONSISTENCY: repeated execution pattern; methods may change.
- SELF.PERSISTENCE: pursue a valid goal through setbacks with method flexibility.
- DECISION.GOAL_DISCIPLINE: commitment to goal/problem, not preferred solution.
- LITERACY.DISCIPLINE: conclusion strength proportional to evidence quality, scope, and currency.

Conceptual synthesis may precede complete evidence, but empirical or causal claims remain blocked until their source packet and boundaries are reviewed.

# Chapter 28 QA — Kepentingan, Kekuasaan, dan Pengaruh

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Power is treated as organizational literacy, not as a goal to maximize.

2. **Authority versus influence — PASS**  
   Formal authority is separated from expertise, information, resources, relationships, and network position.

3. **Relational/contextual power — PASS**  
   Dependency and alternatives are used to explain why influence varies by situation.

4. **Interest versus bad faith — PASS**  
   Different interests are not automatically interpreted as unethical motives.

5. **Perspective boundary — PASS**  
   Suspected motives remain hypotheses and must not be converted into facts without evidence.

6. **Ethical boundary — PASS**  
   Deception, coercion, retaliation, abuse of authority, concealed conflicts of interest, and exploitation of vulnerability are explicitly bounded.

7. **Influence versus manipulation — PASS**  
   Persuasion, negotiation, coalition-building, expertise, and authority are not collapsed into manipulation.

8. **Boundary with Chapter 27 — PASS**  
   Chapter 27 maps the organizational system; Chapter 28 maps power/influence within that system.

9. **Boundary with Chapters 29–30 — PASS**  
   Chapter 29 owns interest conflict; Chapter 30 owns manipulation and defensive literacy.

10. **Practical framework — PASS**  
    The Power & Influence Map is explicitly a book synthesis, not a validated power taxonomy.

11. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliography verification for power bases, dependence, resource control, networks, information asymmetry, and ethical influence.

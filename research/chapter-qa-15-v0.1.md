# Chapter 15 QA — Feedback dan Percakapan Sulit

Status: **PASS WITH REVISIONS**

## Findings

1. **Leader capability framing — PASS**  
   Feedback is treated as judgment, information exchange, accountability, and learning rather than blunt criticism.

2. **Boundary with Chapter 10 — PASS**  
   Listening is not redefined; the chapter uses listening as one component of difficult conversations.

3. **Boundary with Chapter 11 — PASS**  
   Respect remains the relational principle. Chapter 15 operationalizes it in corrective and evaluative conversations.

4. **Boundary with Chapter 12 — PASS**  
   Language/tension reduction remains Chapter 12; Chapter 15 owns feedback purpose, evidence, request, response, and follow-up.

5. **Boundary with Chapter 14 — PASS**  
   Audience adaptation remains Chapter 14. Chapter 15 focuses on the feedback/difficult-conversation process across audiences.

6. **Boundary with Chapters 17–20 — PASS WITH GUARDRAIL**  
   Conflict, de-escalation, boundaries, and repair remain later chapters. Chapter 15 only introduces when a conversation needs to pause or shift to another mechanism.

7. **Mechanism — PASS**  
   The chapter consistently moves from purpose to observable evidence, impact, context, request, response, and follow-up.

8. **Evidence posture — PASS WITH PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
   Evidence is used to establish that feedback effects are heterogeneous and context-dependent. No universal feedback formula is claimed.

9. **Accountability — PASS**  
   Listening to context does not automatically remove responsibility; psychological safety is explicitly separated from absence of standards or consequences.

10. **Practical framework — PASS**  
    `Purpose → Observation → Impact → Perspective → Request → Response → Agreement/Decision → Follow-up` is explicitly marked as a book synthesis, not a validated taxonomy.

11. **Anti-manipulation boundary — PASS**  
    The chapter rejects humiliation, personal retaliation, forced agreement, and feedback used to delay necessary decisions.

12. **Red-team — PASS WITH MINOR PROVENANCE CONDITION**  
    Final publication requires bibliographic verification and preservation of design/sample limitations for cited evidence.

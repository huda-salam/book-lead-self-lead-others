# Chapter Briefs v0.2 — Memimpin Diri, Memimpin Sesama

> Status: Red-team revised. Ready for chapter-level evidence mapping and retrieval testing; not yet approved for substantive drafting.

## Part I — Memimpin Diri
1. **Siapa yang Sebenarnya Mengendalikan Respons Saya?** — SELF.RESPONSE; stimulus, interpretasi, emosi, respons, agency; drill response log.
2. **Ego, Emosi, dan Jeda** — SELF.EGO_EMOTION; status threat, defensiveness, regulation; drill what-am-I-protecting → pause → label → choose.
3. **Rendah Hati Tanpa Merendahkan Diri** — SELF.HUMILITY; accurate self-view, appreciation, teachability; drill receive correction.
4. **Tetap Berdiri Ketika Keadaan Tidak Sesuai Harapan** — SELF.RESILIENCE; recovery, adaptive persistence; controllable/influenceable/uncontrollable.
5. **Positive, Tetapi Tidak Menipu Diri** — SELF.POSITIVE_REALISM; constructive realism; good/bad/uncertain/changeable.

## Part II — Membaca Situasi dan Memilih Respons
6. **Jangan Langsung Menjawab** — READ.PAUSE; response latency, stakes, timing, proportionality.
7. **Fakta, Tafsir, dan Cerita di Kepala** — READ.OBSERVATION; observation/interpretation/evidence/unknown.
8. **Membaca Orang Tanpa Sok Tahu** — READ.PERSPECTIVE; Observe → Hypothesize → Ask → Test → Update.
9. **Memilih Respons: Bicara, Diam, Bertanya, Menunda, Menolak, atau Eskalasi** — READ.RESPONSE_CHOICE; stakes, authority, risk, reversibility.

## Part III — Berbicara dan Menghargai Sesama
10. **Mendengar Tanpa Ikut Terbakar** — COMM.LISTENING; listening, validation, emotional stability; acknowledge → test → next step.
11. **Menghargai dan Berbeda Pendapat** — COMM.RESPECT; dignity, contribution, status bias, substantive disagreement.
12. **Bahasa yang Menurunkan Tegangan** — COMM.LANGUAGE; diction, softening, metaphor, directness.
13. **Humor, Cletukan, dan Batas yang Aman** — COMM.HUMOR; target, context, hierarchy, timing, reversibility.
14. **Berbicara ke Atasan, Rekan, dan Bawahan** — COMM.AUDIENCE; authority, expectations, briefing, adaptation.
15. **Feedback dan Percakapan Sulit** — COMM.FEEDBACK; observation, impact, relevance, action, response, follow-up.

## Part IV — Hubungan, Konflik, dan Batas
16. **Trust, Psychological Safety, Accountability, dan Respect** — REL.RELATIONAL_CONDITIONS; distinguish relational conditions.
17. **Konflik: Jangan Romantisasi, Jangan Takuti** — REL.CONFLICT; task, relationship, process, status conflict.
18. **Menurunkan Suhu Konflik** — REL.DESCALATION; pause, reframing, clarification, boundaries.
19. **Batas: Tegas Tanpa Menjadi Kasar** — REL.BOUNDARY; refusal, limits, consequences, escalation.
20. **Memperbaiki Hubungan Setelah Kesalahan** — REL.REPAIR; acknowledgement, apology, correction, restitution, follow-up.

## Part V — Dari Masalah ke Solusi
21. **Keluhan Bukan Selalu Masalah** — PROBLEM.FRAME; signal, symptom, problem, stakeholder.
22. **Jangan Jatuh Cinta pada Solusi Pertama** — PROBLEM.HYPOTHESES; hypothesis, causes, constraints, options.
23. **Mencari Akar Tanpa Terjebak Root Cause** — PROBLEM.CAUSAL; causal reasoning with uncertainty.
24. **Memilih Solusi dan Mengakui Trade-off** — DECISION.TRADEOFF; benefits, costs, risk, reversibility, second-order effects.
25. **Dari Keputusan ke Eksekusi** — DECISION.EXECUTION; owner, next action, dependency, deadline, feedback.
26. **Ketika Keputusan Tidak Menghasilkan Outcome yang Diinginkan** — DECISION.REVIEW; decision quality vs outcome quality; avoid hindsight bias.

## Part VI — Dinamika Organisasi dan Pengaruh
27. **Organisasi Bukan Hanya Struktur Resmi** — ORG.SYSTEM; networks, incentives, information, norms.
28. **Kepentingan, Kekuasaan, dan Pengaruh** — ORG.POWER_INFLUENCE; interests, incentives, information asymmetry, credibility.
29. **Ketika Kepentingan Bertabrakan** — ORG.INTEREST_CONFLICT; alignment, negotiation, trade-offs, coalition.
30. **Manipulasi, Framing, dan Permainan Psikologis** — ORG.DEFENSE; deception, pressure, selective information, scapegoating; defensive literacy only.

## Part VII — Memimpin Tanpa Menunggu Jabatan
31. **Ownership dan Kredibilitas** — LEAD.OWNERSHIP_CREDIBILITY; responsibility, reliability, competence, integrity, consistency.
32. **Mempengaruhi Tanpa Memaksa** — LEAD.INFLUENCE; credibility, framing, evidence, coalition, reciprocity.
33. **Membuat Orang Lain Bertumbuh** — LEAD.DEVELOPMENT; empowerment, delegation, feedback, autonomy, support.
34. **Memimpin Saat Tekanan Tinggi** — LEAD.PRESSURE; judgment and relational stability under pressure.

## Part VIII — Management dan Strategic Thinking
35. **Tidak Semua Hal Harus Dikerjakan** — MGMT.PRIORITY; focus, value, urgency, opportunity cost.
36. **Delegasi Bukan Memindahkan Beban** — MGMT.DELEGATION; outcome, authority, checkpoints, capability fit.
37. **Melihat Dua Langkah ke Depan** — STRAT.SECOND_ORDER; dependencies, unintended consequences, scenarios.
38. **Strategi Berarti Memilih** — STRAT.CHOICE; focus, trade-offs, what not to do.
39. **Mengubah Rencana Ketika Dunia Berubah** — STRAT.ADAPTATION; assumptions, signals, review, update.

## Part IX — Belajar dari Manusia
40. **Cara Membaca Sebuah Kasus** — CASE.REASONING; Context → Facts → Hypotheses → Action → Mechanism → Evidence → Outcome → Limits → Transfer.
41. **Belajar dari Nabi dan Sahabat** — CASE.ISLAMIC_EXEMPLARS; Qur’an, hadith, classical scholarship, historical sources; context and source discipline.
42. **Belajar dari Pemimpin, Tokoh Sejarah, Business Leaders, Profesional, dan Orang Biasa** — CASE.HUMAN_EXEMPLARS; behavior, context, mechanism hypothesis, evidence, transfer boundary.
43. **Belajar dari Kegagalan** — CASE.FAILURE; mechanism extraction without hindsight distortion.

## Part X — Sistem Latihan
44. **Personal Operating System: Response, Practice, Review, Habit** — PRACTICE.OS; personal response protocol, response drills, language drills, humility/respect drills, implementation intentions, review, feedback, maintenance.
